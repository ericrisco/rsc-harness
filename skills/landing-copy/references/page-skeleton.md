# Page skeleton and evidence-led example

Choose sections by the buyer's unanswered questions. For a VSL page, also read
`vsl-page-handoff.md`; the video and readable text carry the same offer revision.

## Section decisions

| Question | Content | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is this for me? | Clear headline, audience, supported benefit, primary action | Buyer research and approved offer |
| Why consider it? | Relevant problem or current alternative; shorten for informed visitors | Research, distinguished from assumptions |
| What do I receive? | Included items, scope, prerequisites and cost | Approved offer revision |
| Why believe it? | Demonstration, permitted testimony or contextualized measured result | Source ID, scope and permission |
| What happens next? | Action, destination, next step and exact conditions | Verified journey or explicit draft requirement |
| What could stop me? | Real objections, answered with known facts | Research and product constraints |

Repeat one primary action at useful decision points. There is no required quota of testimonials.
A guarantee is a condition, not evidence that the product achieves a result. Keep unsupported
claims outside public copy and state what evidence or decision would resolve them.

## Worked example: fictional fixture, verified behavior supplied

Assume the task supplies these facts: a migration preview flags duplicate accounts before writing
live records; importing requires an approver; demo E1 uses synthetic records; the action is booking
a walkthrough. There are no customer results, delivery-time measurements or published prices.

```text
HEADLINE
Review import exceptions before going live

SUBHEAD
For teams migrating account records: see flagged duplicates before an approver authorizes the import.

ACTION
Book a walkthrough

WHAT YOU CAN INSPECT
The preview identifies records requiring review. Your approver decides whether the mapping is ready.

PRODUCT DEMONSTRATION
Watch the preview flag duplicate accounts in synthetic test data, then show the approval step.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT
Book a walkthrough to see the process and discuss your migration requirements.
```

Production note: capture E1 and verify the booking destination before publication. The example
supports preview and approval behavior. It does not support a claim that errors are eliminated or
migration finishes within a particular time. A source ad promising those outcomes must be corrected,
not echoed to preserve message match. Private source IDs belong in the handoff, not necessarily in
public-facing copy.
